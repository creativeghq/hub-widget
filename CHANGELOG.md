# What's new

Newest first. Each release's GitHub page opens with its entry from this file.

## 1.4.9 — 4 Nov 2026

- **New app icon.** The materials&decor logo, filling the whole icon. This is what you see on the
  notifications that slide in from the side of the screen, and in Finder and System Settings. The
  top bar icon stays as it was.

## 1.4.8 — 4 Nov 2026

- **Real glass icons.** The tiles are now see-through, with a diagonal reflection and a bright edge
  like the macOS glass, and the shadow around each box is gone. Panels got the same glass.
- **Appointments fits.** The longest app name no longer gets cut off on the smaller widget.

## 1.4.7 — 3 Nov 2026

- **New: Appointments.** Your appointments as their own app, after Tasks: how many wait for you to
  confirm (badged), today's and the next 7 days', each with its time and service. Today's also
  appear in Today.
- **Icons in the macOS style.** Each area's icon is now a frosted glass tile — light, or graphite in
  dark mode — with its symbol in the area's colour, like the apps in your Dock. Badges are smaller.

## 1.4.6 — 3 Nov 2026

- **The widget is a home screen.** Every area is an app icon with its name underneath, in its own
  colour. A red badge shows how many things need you, like on iPhone. Click an icon to go inside and
  see the figures and the list.
- **New: Analytics.** Sales this month against the same days last month, money in and money out,
  sales per day for the last two weeks as a chart, and your top customers this month. The figures
  come from Finance → Reports, so both always agree.
- **macOS glass design.** Panels, buttons and icons use the translucent look of macOS Tahoe, and follow
  the desktop's tinted and clear widget styles.

## 1.4.5 — 3 Nov 2026

- **Every notification shows which area it is about.** In the menu-bar panel each notification has a
  coloured icon in front of it: Finance, Inbox, CRM, Jobs, Marketing, Projects and so on.
- **Mac banners name the area** under the title, and Notification Center groups them by area.

## 1.4.4 — 3 Nov 2026

- **My tasks and Leads on top.** The large Hub overview opens with your tasks and your newest leads as
  lists side by side. Tick a task's circle to complete it. Every other section sits under them as
  tiles in two columns.
- **Task board with day filters.** Tap My tasks to see your tasks as cards, filtered by
  **All · Overdue · Today · Tomorrow · This week · Later**, each with its count.

## 1.4.3 — 3 Nov 2026

- **One key for the app and every widget.** The key saved in the app's Settings now reaches the
  widgets too. A widget still holding an old key falls back to the app's key instead of saying
  "API key was refused".

## 1.4.2 — 3 Nov 2026

- **MH logo in the menu bar**, with the unread count next to it.
- **New lead from the menu bar shows every field** — the panel resizes to the form.
- **A refused API key is said out loud** instead of quietly showing old figures.
- **Mac notifications work again** — the time the app sent lost its `+` on the way, so every check
  was refused.

## 1.4.1 — 3 Nov 2026

- **Everything stays in the menu bar.** + Lead opens a panel from the menu-bar icon instead of a
  separate window.
- The **+ Lead** button always shows when you can see CRM.

## 1.4.0 — 3 Nov 2026

- **+ Lead** — add a lead from the widget in a few seconds.
- **Tick off tasks** straight from the widget.
- **Today** — one tile with everything due today or late, from every area.
- **Real estate** enquiries and viewings, and every section now fits the widget.

## 1.3.0 — 2 Nov 2026

- **Clearer design.** Tiles look like buttons, lists look like lists, and text sizes are easier to
  read.

## 1.2.0 — 2 Nov 2026

- **Click a section to see its list** inside the widget; click a row to open the record.
- **Mac notifications** for everything your platform bell gets.
- **Updates itself** in the background.
- The widget's design comes from the platform, so it can change without reinstalling.

## 1.0.0 — 2 Nov 2026

- First release: Hub overview and Hub section widgets for your workspace.
